Can I get only Reels editing without Meta Ads? +

Yes. Editing Only is a separate monthly package at ₹17,999 for 15 Reels. Meta Ads management is not included.

Who provides the footage? +

The client provides the raw footage, product assets, brand guidelines and any required references unless a separate shoot or production arrangement is agreed.

Will you guarantee leads or sales? +

No. We agree on the work and measurement plan, then report the actual data. Outcomes depend on factors including offer, audience, budget, landing page and market conditions.

Do you build apps and maintain existing websites? +

Yes, project scope is confirmed first. App development is quoted after discovery; website maintenance starts from ₹2,500/month for an agreed list of tasks and exclusions.

Not yet. It is marked Coming Soon and will be offered only after the service is built and tested.
